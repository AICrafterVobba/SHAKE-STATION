## 🌌 About The Project
**Shake Station** is an immersive, educational web game and junior astronaut mission trainer designed for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**. Set in Gale Crater, Mars, players take charge of keeping their astronaut buddy (**Pip**) safe through a 10-sol mission simulation while managing solar energy, radiation shields, life support, and navigating real-world space weather hazards.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- **10-Sol Mission Simulation:** Experience dynamic day-to-day survival challenges on Mars.
- **NASA Data Integration:** Powered by real NASA telemetry including **Curiosity RAD** radiation baselines and **DONKI / SDO** solar flare data.
- **Interactive Weather & Hazards:** Survive hazardous dust storms, hazy skies, and sudden X-class solar flares with variable signal delay from Earth.
- **Dynamic Mood System:** Pip’s facial expressions and emotional states react dynamically to your resource management choices and health status.
- **Immersive Web Audio & Visuals:** Custom Web Audio sound effects for button clicks, coin additions, solar flares, dust storms, and ambient Martian atmosphere.
- **Daily Postcard Logs:** Unlock daily postcards containing verified NASA science discoveries and mission logs after each Sol.

## 🛠️ Built With
- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3 (Glassmorphism & Sci-Fi Cockpit UI), Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
- **Audio:** Native Web Audio API & HTML5 Audio Elements
- **Data Sources:** NASA DONKI API, NASA SDO, Curiosity Rover RAD (SwRI)

---

## 🎮 How to Play
1. **Read the Sky:** Check daily weather forecasts and delayed Earth communication warnings.
2. **Allocate Sun Coins:** Budget your energy across Life Support, Food Crops, Radiation Shields, and Science discoveries. Unspent coins roll over into your battery reserve.
3. **Shake & Survive:** Click *"Shake & End Sol"* to process the day's events and keep Pip safe for 10 full Sols!
